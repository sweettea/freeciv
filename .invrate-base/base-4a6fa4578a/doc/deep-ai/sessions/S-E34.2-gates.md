## THE JOB

Gates 2, 3 and 4 from W34 A.2, each a ratio-multiplier on an EXISTING want,
each with its own execution counter, each with its own A/B. Everything in
this epic is reachable only by PINNING — FREE mode stays identity, and the
epic ends with the byte-identity gate still green.

- **Gate 2 — operation formation** (`deep_op_form*`, `deepops.c`). Low
  `mil` forms assaults only against opportunistic or threatening targets;
  high `mil` forms freely. One gain: `stance_op_gain`.
- **Gate 3 — build wants** (the `horizon_*` credits in `deepwant`). The
  growth ladder against barracks/walls. Gain: `stance_growth_gain`.
- **Gate 4 — expansion** (`settler_rush_weight`, `colony_lift_weight`
  consumers). High `econ` expands while land remains. Gain:
  `stance_expand_gain`.

Shape rule from A.2, non-negotiable: `want *= f(stance)` where `f` is
identity when the axes are equal, and gate 1's survival floor precedent
holds — no gate may push a want below an existing survival floor. No new
want scales (RC4).
