# The T50/T100 panel, first readings (2026-10-27)

`benchmark/panel.py` prints per-seat doctrine milestones at fixed turns
from scorelog tags every arm already logs, paired by seed. No new games.
Proposed by `theory-of-play-2026-10-26.md` §H. Four readings below; the
third is the first direct deep-with-stack-on vs baseline comparison the
project has (same 20 holdout seeds, same maps — the stacked identity
step proved `off` reproduces the archived s6 games byte for byte).

`settlers` = units that can `ACTION_FOUND_CITY` (real founders).
`units/city` = `munits` ÷ cities, where `munits` is every non-special
unit (`server/report.c get_munits`) — founders and workers included.
`Monarchy+` = government index ≥ 3 (anything past Tribal/Despotism).
`achievements` = count of ≥ 900-culture steps between samples (a
civ2civ3 achievement credits 1000 at once).

**Reading.** Deep is *ahead* at T50 (score +1.5, pop +1.3, bigger
cities) and loses the game between T50 and T100: the city gap goes
from +0.1 to −0.74 in those fifty turns, exactly the T26–75 founding
band the city ledger named. Three things are visible at T50 that were
not in any end-of-game table: deep's units per city are **3× baseline's**
(1.02 vs 0.33 — the "7×" was T16; by T50 it is 3× and still there at
T150), deep's treasury is a quarter of baseline's and never enters the
150–500 band (0–2% of seats vs 28–63%), and baseline is already carrying
**1.4 live settlers a seat** to deep's 0.6 — 2.9 vs 0.5 by T100. Baseline
also books about four times as many achievements by T100 (0.17 vs 0.02
per seat ≈ +3 points) and +135 culture (≈ +3 points): the theory memo's
"unclaimed 20-point lumps" are ~6 of the 6.85-point gap at T150 on this
sample, and nobody had looked. Deep adopts Monarchy *more* than
baseline at T100 (67% vs 57%) — government is not the problem.

## 2. Contested, training seeds 1–100: stack off → on (n = 100)

| | T50 off | T50 on | Δ ± se | T100 off | T100 on | Δ ± se | T150 off | T150 on | Δ ± se |

| pop | 8.7 | 8.1 | **−0.59 ± 0.10** | 21.1 | 21.3 | +0.26 ± 0.27 | 26.0 | 27.1 | +1.05 ± 0.35 |
| pop/city | 3.08 | 2.54 | **−0.54 ± 0.04** | 5.24 | 5.08 | −0.16 ± 0.05 | 5.95 | 5.95 | 0.00 |
| settlers | 0.76 | 1.73 | **+0.97 ± 0.05** | 0.42 | 2.21 | **+1.79 ± 0.08** | 0.16 | 1.86 | +1.70 ± 0.07 |
| units/city | 1.07 | 1.00 | −0.07 ± 0.03 | 2.93 | 2.60 | −0.33 ± 0.08 | 3.85 | 3.42 | −0.43 ± 0.10 |
| score | 20.8 | 20.3 | **−0.58 ± 0.17** | 56.2 | 56.3 | +0.12 ± 0.54 | 82.6 | 85.0 | +2.41 ± 1.00 |

**Reading.** The stack's foundings arrive *early* (+0.23 cities at T50,
t 9.8) and are paid for in parent-city population (−0.59 pop, 2-pop
settlers), so the on arm is *behind* on score at T50 and only ahead at
T150. The settler inventory is **not** a late-game artefact: +0.97 live
settlers at T50, +1.79 at T100. The stack restores deep's founder stock
to roughly baseline's level from T50 on (1.73 vs baseline's 1.42; 2.21
vs 2.93) — and the cities do not follow at baseline's rate (4.45 vs
5.10 at T100). **After the stack, the binding constraint is site
acceptance, not settler supply**: the ledger's "72% of deep's T51–75
searches find no legal tile" is a deep-side refusal (baseline finds a
tile on the same map), and it is where the next epic belongs.

**Reading.** With the five gates on, deep trails baseline by **4.15 ±

and by 0.47 cities (was 0.64). n = 20 and reporting-only, so this is a
direction, not a number — but it is the first time deep-with-anything-on
has been put beside baseline, and it says the stack closes roughly
40% of the gap. What it does not touch: the 3× unit count, the
treasury, the achievements.

## 4. lategame-80 (econ), T40 / T80

Deep gates-off → baseline, holdout (n = 20): cities +0.90 ± 0.17 / +1.02
± 0.22; **settlers 0.87 vs 7.10 / 0.73 vs 6.25**; pop/city 4.94 vs 4.12;
units/city 1.34 vs 0.60 at T40; gold 52 vs 142; score −1.52 ± 0.85 /
**+7.33 ± 2.48**. Stack off → on, training (n = 100): cities +0.44 ±
0.05 / +0.62 ± 0.07; settlers +1.75 / +1.56; score +1.52 ± 0.44 / +3.87
± 0.64.

**Reading.** On the econ venue baseline carries **seven live founders a
seat at T40** (every seat starts with eight; baseline founds 7.5 cities
and *still* holds 7.1 — it keeps building them) against deep's 0.9, and
founds a city more. Baseline's cities are smaller (4.12 vs 4.94 pop) —
many small cities, a permanent settler stock, and it wins. Deep's
"fewer, bigger, no stock" is the losing shape on both venues. Note that
baseline's stock is itself wasteful (2 pop each, idle); what wins is not
the stock but the founding rate that the stock evidently serves.

## What this changes

1. **The achievements and culture gap is real and unpriced** (~6 of
   6.85 points at T150 on contested, holdout). `Map_Known` is explorers;
   `Multicultural` is one conquest; both are cheap. No deep mechanism
   knows they exist (theory memo §G).
2. **The next expansion lever is site acceptance, not supply**, and it is
   a deep-only refusal (W05's TB_IGNORE border wall, the site scorer's
   thresholds, crowding prices) — the "self-inflicted deficit audit".
3. **The 3× army is visible at T50 and persists**; it is the treasury
   bill (1 gold/unit) and the shields that were not settlers. The
   empty-city defender switch (knob inventory D) is the first suspect.
4. The panel should run on every cell from now on; it costs nothing.
