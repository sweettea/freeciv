# The build-want calibration corpus, re-taken (2026-08-06)

The July 2026 corpus (W01, 10 contested games, 45k decisions) quantified
the scale disease and gated three conversions on the building anchor
becoming honest. Five sessions of horizon credits, gold-upkeep charging,
the growth ladder, the bug round and the production-switch price later,
this is the re-take that decides whether those gates are open.

## Capture method

Binary PINNED at HEAD `4787233332` built with `-Dadvchoicetrack=true`
(sha256 `d2274419b6ee…`, server + `libfreeciv.so`, `--server`), so the
`adv_choice` producer tag exists. `--arms deep`, `TUNING=benchmark/
tuning/identity.ini`, 20 seeds per cell on `contested.conf` (civ2civ3,
8 players, T150) and `isolated-150.conf` (civ2civ3, 4 players, T150,
migration). Results in `benchmark/results/R3-corpus/`; the table is
regenerated by `benchmark/wantcensus.py RESULTS_DIR`.

**The census build is behaviour-neutral**: 10/10 smoke scorelogs
bit-identical to the default build of the same commit
(`52e293e7aba5`), hashes `bc553467c513 6e3885799d7c d39705b3f05b
88aad2316372 61e2bf59ba57 190d8c2520ff ca4b939713f9 ffadfe351823
c5ea7fcdd388 d28ccfcfd0d6`.

Two lines feed it:

- **`deep-want`**, one per final build decision — the same
  winner-conditioned line the July corpus used, so the two are
  comparable.
- **`deep-bid`** (new, `4787233332`), one per city-turn — what the
  founder, the worker, the building anchor and deep's military choice
  each bid, **win or lose**. A winner-conditioned sample cannot price a
  class against a class it rarely beats, and founder-versus-building is
  exactly that pair. Every value is already computed at the seam; the
  building anchor is the same maximum over `building_want[]` that
  `building_advisor_choose()` takes, recomputed without the AI callback
  so the census cannot perturb what it watches.

Wants are reported **pre-trait**: `domestic_advisor_choose_build()`
multiplies the founder by EXPANSIONIST and the building by
`0.5 + BUILDER/2` afterwards, both 1.0 at default traits.

**The settler saturates.** `DAI_WANT_DOMESTIC_MAX` is 750.075, and
**32.8% (contested) / 51.9% (isolated-150) of all winning founder wants
are pinned exactly there.** On isolated-150 the founder's p50, p90 and
max are all the clamp. A want that spends half its life at the ceiling
has no resolution left: it cannot say that one site is better than
another, cannot respond to a rival's pressure, and cannot lose to a
building it should lose to. That is a measurement, not an
interpretation, and it is the single strongest argument in this
document for deflating the founder.

**The explorer never won anything.** 0.1 / 0.3 wins per game, at a want
of 7.5-30 against an anchor of 61.5-104.6. W01 predicted the explorer
would follow founder deflation rather than self-inflation; the corpus
says its bid is 10-20x below the anchor and no rescale of the founder
can reach it. Explorer re-pricing is NOT unblocked by this session.

**The trade caravan wins at want 1.0** — the fallback rung, 0.2 / 1.6
times a game, which is R2's result restated: the trade producer is a
non-participant in this auction and the wonder producer (p50 132-137)
is the caravan claimant, exactly as R2b measured.

## 3. The finding this session did not go looking for

W01 replaced priority-by-magnitude with an explicit TIER so a must-act
want would outrank a merely large one. **Inside the emergency tier,
priority is still a magnitude**, and the corpus says the tier holds two
populations that are not in the same currency.

A `kill_something_with` attacker at 259 976 outranks a grave-danger
first defender at 100 by three orders of magnitude, in a tier whose
entire purpose was to stop exactly that. July's "Trireme max 63505" was
read as a normal-tier spike; it is not. It rides the emergency tier,
where deep's tier discipline never looks.

The honest emergency population (defenders, police, bodyguards,
defensive diplomats, defence buildings) has **p99 = 300.0 in both
cells** — the value W01 calibrated for the defensive diplomat — with a
max of 353 (contested) and 542 (isolated). **15.5% / 16.4% of all
emergency-tier wins exceed 400**, and essentially all of them are
attacker, ferry, veterancy-building or offensive-diplomat lines.

`emergency_value_cap` (`3a86052ba3`) is the instrument: it clamps the
VALUE and never the tier, so every must-act want still outranks every
normal one and a defender in grave danger keeps the number it had.
400.0 is above the honest p99 in both cells and below the 10th
percentile of every spike class.

Two consequences the cap has, stated because they are consequences and
not side effects:

- **The offensive diplomat is clipped too.** Its emergency values
  (p50 695 / 1128) are deep's own expected-value wants, which W01
  deliberately left uncalibrated as honest. A uniform cap treats them
  as a spike. The alternative — capping only the attacker and ferry
  classes — is more surgical and more arbitrary; the uniform form is
  the one that states the tier is a tier.
- **The veterancy building is a spike class and nobody knew.** 9.5-10.4
  emergency wins a game at p50 ~4 000 and a max of 129 488, filed as
  CT_BUILDING. It is not in any workstream.

## 4. What a deflator can and cannot be derived for

    class        contested   isolated-150   shipped   basis
    settler          0.154          0.273      0.20    median match, both cells
    worker           2.48           3.75       3.0     median match, both cells
    building          1.0            1.0       1.0     ANCHOR, no key
    defender         0.29           2.99       1.0     NO DERIVATION
    attacker         1.10           1.96       1.0     NO DERIVATION
    ferry            0.65           3.22       1.0     NO DERIVATION
    caravan             —              —       n/a     owned by R2 / R2b

The two economy classes agree in sign and rank across two rulesets'
worth of different geography, and the shipped value is the middle of
their two answers — the same reconciliation R2b used to take ~5 from
4.3 and 5.6.

**The three military classes have no derivable scale and ship at 1.0.**
Their ratios to the anchor are winner-conditioned (deep's military
choice is a single merged bid, so the bid census cannot split it) and
they INVERT between cells: the defender wants 0.29 on contested and
2.99 on isolated-150. A number that changes direction with the venue is
not a calibration, and rule 7 says say so rather than pick one. They
stay registered, at 1.0, for the cloud pile.

The caravan classes are deliberately absent: R2 measured the trade
producer's deflator at ~8 and R2b the wonder producer's at ~5, both
already implemented as `trade_merit_gain` / `wonder_merit_gain` at the
same seam. A `scale_caravan` here would double-count them.

## 5. What is NOT in this corpus

- **Per-producer military bids.** Deep forms one military choice; the
  census sees the merged winner. Splitting defender from attacker from
  ferry at bid time needs a per-producer line inside
  `deep_military_choose_build()`, which is the instrument combat E2
  will want anyway.
- **The runner-up.** `domestic_advisor_choose_build()` returns its
  winner; the building want a settler beat is visible (the anchor is
  logged unconditionally) but the caravan and wonder bids it beat are
  not. R2b's clamp gap is the same hole.
- **Traits.** Both cells run default traits, so the trait multipliers
  average to 1.0 and the corpus cannot see the currency corruption W01
  documented (`daidomestic.c:620`, `:558`). A traits cell would show a
  wider settler spread, not a different median.
