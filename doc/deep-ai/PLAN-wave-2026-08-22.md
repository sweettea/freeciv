## 2. Paradox A — "defaults beat pv3 on econ, but no block carries it"

State: autopsy round 1 measured full-pv3 −98.8 (t −2.59) vs compiled
defaults on 8 *training* seeds, econ venue; war venue did not
reproduce (+28.9). Two rounds of family ablation named G/GCM; the
holdout confirm then showed the GCM drop is worth −1.4, not +24. So
either the pv3-vs-defaults gap is (i) a training-seed artefact, (ii)
real but interaction-driven (no single family carries it), or (iii)
real and carried by the un-ablated remainder.

**The resolving test is one cheap run that was never done: the
defaults arm itself on HOLDOUT seeds.** `--report-holdout` with the
zero-key defaults ini, isolated-150, seeds 101-116 (pv3's holdout
games are cached; baseline cancels arm-vs-arm). 16 games, ~2 h.

- defaults−pv3 ≈ 0 on holdout → the headline was a training artefact;
  the paradox dissolves exactly the way GCM did; pv3 stands; stop all
  subtractive-ablation work permanently.
- defaults−pv3 > 0 persists (report mean AND median AND signs — the
  pv4 confirm showed two-seed tails flipping means) → the gap is real
  but non-decomposable → the correct next act is not ablation but a
  fresh tune seeded *from defaults* on the econ venue, treating pv3 as
  a local optimum with baked-in interactions.

Verdict either way closes the campaign's last open tuning question.

## 3. Paradox B — "wins openings, survives fine, loses the war score"

Not a paradox after decomposition: deep built a defensive army and an
insurance economy, and the score metric pays kills (15% of tech50war
score), expansion, and the compounding downstream of both. The open
question is only *why employment is −33%*. The in-flight probe lands
four tables (disposition census, attack funnel with decline causes,
op lifecycle, wait ledger) plus the kill-dyad matrix. **Decision tree,
pre-committed:**

| Probe outcome (the number that picks it) | Follow-on epic | A/B design |
|---|---|---|
| **Targets vetoed** — funnel shows targets priced >0 then declined by named gates (sortie_win_floor, op_launch_odds, tired_attack_floor, op_abort_floor, muster waits) at ≫ launch rate | EMPLOY-GATES: relax/knob the single largest named gate only | mixed tech50war, ≥20 mirrored pairs, one knob; primary = kills/built + per-side kill:loss at T40-100 (resolves at n=16, prior |t|>6); score secondary. Kill: kills rise, score falls — the caution was correct; revert and route to allocation instead |
| **Targets absent** — priced-above-zero target count ≈ 0 in the window T40-80 | EMPLOY-VALUATION: attack/conquest pricing recalibration vs stock's kill_desire | same venue/metrics; diagnose-first (why zero: odds model? value weights? visibility?) |
| **Classic farms classics** — dyad matrix: classic-on-classic kills/built ≥ ~1.5× deep-on-classic | Deficit is partly environmental; deep's caution near-correct vs its real opportunity set. De-prioritize employment; the war-venue play becomes expansion + citizens/techs (ranks 3-4); record that out-killing classic requires *being* classic |
| **Deep dies in the open** — classic-on-deep conversion high with deep losses concentrated outside cities/forts | EXPOSURE epic: movement discipline / stack hygiene; the risk-routing family (W12H) graduates from flavour to score track | mixed venue, primary = per-side losses; the soft-borders risk pricer is the reusable machinery |

Mixed outcomes get the union, executed one epic at a time, largest
number first. The allocation leg (rank 2, −13%) is actionable
regardless of branch: Engineers 3.6x, Destroyers +20, scale_worker
1.95 vs the §7 par finding — a worker/military allocation tune has
evidence independent of the employment answer, but runs AFTER the
employment verdict so the two do not share an attribution window.
